FAQ

What games can I play on a school Chromebook?

Snake, 2048, Breakout, Memory Match, and Tic-Tac-Toe are reliable, light browser games.

Do these games need a download?

No. Every game in our library runs directly in Chrome.

Are these games safe for school?

Yes. They are simple puzzle and arcade games with no chat, no accounts, and no personal data collection beyond local high scores.
