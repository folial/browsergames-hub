Best activities to play over video call

These need no game site at all — just a Zoom, Google Meet, or FaceTime call and one person sharing their screen or reading prompts.

  • Pictionary / Drawful style: use a free drawing app or the Zoom whiteboard. One draws, the other guesses against a 90-second timer.
  • Charades: a browser word generator gives the prompt, one person acts, the other guesses.
  • Trivia: host a private Kahoot room and build a short quiz about your relationship.
  • Would You Rather: a free question generator with increasingly difficult choices. Simple but surprisingly revealing.
  • 20 Questions: no tools needed. Keep score on a shared note.

If you're willing to download

  • Stardew Valley: a low-pressure co-op option for couples. One-time purchase; check the official system requirements before buying.
  • It Takes Two: built entirely for two players. Only one of you needs to own it.
  • Words With Friends 2: slow, turn-based word games that keep a conversation running all week.

How to set up a long-distance game night

  1. Pick one main game and one warm-up game from the table above.
  2. Start a Discord, Zoom, or FaceTime call and keep it running for the whole session.
  3. Send all room links or invites before you start so nobody sits in silence.
  4. Set a loose timebox of 60–90 minutes. It is easier to extend than to fade out awkwardly.
  5. End with something low-stakes — Connect 4, a quiz, or a shared video — so the night ends on a laugh.

FAQ

What games can long-distance couples play online?

Skribbl.io, Codenames Online, Board Game Arena, Chess.com, Connect 4, Kosmi, and Minecraft Classic are the fastest free browser options.

How do I play games with my long-distance girlfriend or boyfriend?

Start a voice or video call, pick one free browser game, create a private room, and share the link. Keep the game simple so you can talk while playing.

What are the best free online games for couples?

Drawing and word games (Skribbl.io, Codenames), quick versus games (Connect 4, Chess), and party rooms (Kosmi) are the best free options.

Can we play browser games on phones?

Many of the external options have mobile-friendly pages, but support varies by game and browser. Check the official page before planning a session.

What games can couples play without downloading?

All ten games in the comparison table are no-download browser games. Skribbl.io and Connect 4 are the fastest to start.
