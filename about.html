About BrowserGames Hub

A small, independent collection of free browser games and practical guides for people who want to play without downloads or accounts.

What we make

BrowserGames Hub builds lightweight pixel games that run directly in a modern browser. The current library focuses on short sessions: arcade challenges, puzzles, memory games, word games, and same-screen two-player games.

We also publish guides that help you choose a game by group size, device, setup time, and play style. External games are recommendations, not games hosted by BrowserGames Hub, and should be labeled and linked to their current official destination.

What we care about

  • Clear descriptions instead of exaggerated claims.
  • Fast starts on phones, Chromebooks, and older laptops where practical.
  • Simple controls, visible feedback, and a usable keyboard path.
  • Honest separation between original on-site games and external recommendations.

Editorial independence

BrowserGames Hub is an independent project. A future advertising or affiliate relationship will not change the labels, rankings, or limitations described in a guide. When a recommendation is sponsored or earns a commission, that relationship should be disclosed on the relevant page.

Questions and corrections

If a game is broken, a link has changed, or a guide makes an inaccurate claim, please contact us. We keep a public testing method so readers can understand how recommendations are made.
