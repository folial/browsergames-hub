Pixel Whack-a-Mole

A fast pixel whack-a-mole game. Click or tap the moles before they hide — how many can you get in 30 seconds?

Score: 0 Best: 0 Time: 30

Click or tap a mole before it hides. You have 30 seconds. Restart button resets the game.

How to play Pixel Whack-a-Mole

Click or tap a mole as soon as it appears, then keep scanning the grid for the next one. The round lasts 30 seconds and the counter records how many targets you hit. Restart resets the timer for another reflex challenge.

Scan before you strike

Use your eyes to move across the whole board instead of staring at one hole. A calm rhythm usually produces more hits than random tapping, especially when the targets start changing quickly.
