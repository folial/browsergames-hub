How to Play Slope: Controls, Tips & High Score Guide

Slope is simple to start and hard to master. This guide covers the controls, how the speed works, and the small adjustments that make a big difference in your high score.

How Slope works

You control a ball rolling down an endless neon slope. The longer you survive, the faster the ball moves. You die the moment you hit a wall or fall off the edge. That is the whole game — and exactly why it is so hard to put down.

  • Speed increases continuously with distance, not time.
  • The path narrows and corners appear more often as you go deeper.
  • There are no checkpoints and no power-ups; it is pure reaction.

High score tips that actually work

  1. Stay near the center, not the edges. The ball accelerates faster than you expect; the center gives you the most time to react.
  2. Make small taps, not long presses. Long presses oversteer and throw you into the wall.
  3. Look one second ahead. Focus on where the next gap or wall is, not on the ball.
  4. Do not chase gems. Gems are tempting but they steer you into danger. Survive first, collect later.
  5. Pause to reset your eyes. On long runs, hit P every 30-60 seconds for a two-second break.
  6. Play in full screen. A larger view makes the next turns easier to read.

FAQ

How do you control Slope?

Use the left and right arrow keys, or A and D, to steer the ball.

Why is Slope so hard?

The speed keeps increasing the longer you survive, and one wall hit ends the run.

What is a good Slope score?

For a new player, 20-40 is solid. Breaking 100 usually takes a few hours of practice.

Can you play Slope on a phone?

Yes, most Slope versions support touch by tapping or tilting left and right.
