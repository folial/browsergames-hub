FAQ

What can I play with friends online for free?

Skribbl.io, Gartic Phone, Shell Shockers, Smash Karts, Kosmi, and Codenames Online are the fastest free browser options.

Do my friends need to download anything?

No. Every game in this guide runs in a browser. Some board games are easier with an account, but most work with a room link.

What is the best game for a big group?

Gartic Phone and Kosmi are best for six or more players.
