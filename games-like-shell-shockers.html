FAQ

Is Shell Shockers free?

Yes. The browser version is free at shellshock.io, with optional paid cosmetic items.

What is the closest game to Shell Shockers?

Krunker.io and Venge.io feel closest in terms of quick browser matches and simple controls.

Can these games run on a Chromebook?

Most of them run on a Chromebook in Chrome. Krunker.io and Venge.io are the most reliable on weak hardware.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Every game in this guide runs directly in the browser.
