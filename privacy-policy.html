Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-17. This policy explains what data BrowserGames Hub may collect and how it is used.

Who we are

BrowserGames Hub is a website that offers free browser games, game guides, and game-finder tools. Our website is published at https://browsergames.click.

What data we collect

  • Local storage only: our games store high scores and sound preferences in your browser local storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Server logs: like most websites, the hosting provider may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: we do not serve advertising cookies ourselves. Third-party services such as Google AdSense, Google Analytics, or Cloudflare may set cookies if we enable them. When those services are active, they may use cookies to serve and measure ads.

How we use data

  • To keep the site working and secure
  • To understand which pages are popular
  • To serve relevant advertising if ad services are enabled

Third-party links and embedded games

Some pages may link to external websites (for example, official game pages). Those sites have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or data practices.

Advertising

If we enable advertising, we may use Google AdSense or similar networks. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or visit aboutads.info.

Your rights

  • GDPR (EU/UK): you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, and you may object to processing.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): you may request to know what personal information is collected and to request deletion.

To make a request, contact us at contact@browsergames.click.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
