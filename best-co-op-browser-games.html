FAQ

What is the best free co-op browser game?

Fireboy & Watergirl is the best pure co-op for two players. For bigger groups, Gartic Phone and Bloxd.io are better.

Do we all need an account?

Requirements vary by service. Some let the host share a room link, while others may offer optional or required accounts, so check the linked official page first.

Can we play co-op on school Chromebooks?

Light browser games may work well, but school policies, device limits, and network blocks vary. Try a simple on-site game or check the external game's current requirements.
