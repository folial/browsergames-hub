FAQ

What is the best free co-op browser game?

Fireboy & Watergirl is the best pure co-op for two players. For bigger groups, Gartic Phone and Bloxd.io are better.

Do we all need an account?

No. Almost every game here lets the host create a room and share a link.

Can we play co-op on school Chromebooks?

Fireboy & Watergirl, Gartic Phone, and Krunker.io run well on Chromebooks.
